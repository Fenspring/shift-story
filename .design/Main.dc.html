Culture is just what happens after someone speaks up.

Every unit has already learned whether it is worth saying anything. Shift Story is how you teach it a different answer — one visible follow-through at a time.

Safe enough to say

No names, no logins, no way back to a person. Nothing appears until enough of the unit has answered that no one stands alone in it.

Clear enough to act on

Not a wall of comments to read on your day off. Two or three recurring themes, named plainly, with how many people raised each one.

Visible enough to believe

You said / We did goes back to the whole unit. That is the part that earns you next week’s answers — and the week after that.

Why it matters past the unit

A team that believes it will be heard tells you the small thing on Tuesday, instead of the incident report on Sunday.

Every friction your team carries silently — the missing kit, the handoff that breaks at seven, the workaround nobody wrote down — is time that comes out of patient care. Hearing it earlier is the whole point.

Privacy, by design

Your staff have to believe it before any of this works.

So the guarantees are structural, not promises in a policy. Ask your privacy officer to read this list — it is written for them.

No names, IDs, emails or staff logins No patient information, ever Nothing shown below a response threshold Themes only — leaders never read raw responses Originals deleted once the story is written Safety events routed to your existing channels

Start with one unit. Let the rest ask for it.
