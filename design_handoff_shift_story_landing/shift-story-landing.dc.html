How Shift Story works

01

Ask one question

“What made it harder to deliver a good shift this week?”

Staff respond from a QR code or secure link in under a minute.

02

See the pattern

Shift Story organizes anonymous feedback into recurring operational themes, trends, and de-identified signals.

03

Close the loop

Turn a theme into an action, then share a clear “You said / We did” update with the team.

Feedback is only useful when staff can see what changed.

Shift Story helps leaders earn trust through visible follow-through—not another report.

Staff signal

“Admissions arrive before rooms are ready.”

Recurring theme

Admission flow is the top friction point for the third week.

Leader action

Admission kits stocked in both pods by 6:45 p.m.

Visible update

You said / We did: admission kits are now available in both pods.

Privacy, by design

Safe enough for staff to tell the truth.

Shift Story is designed to protect the signal, not identify the person.

  1. 01No names, employee IDs, emails, or staff logins required
  2. 02No patient information or clinical-event reporting
  3. 03Minimum response thresholds before unit insights appear
  4. 04De-identified themes and comments only
  5. 05No raw-response downloads for leaders
  6. 06Clear routing to existing safety-event and HR channels
